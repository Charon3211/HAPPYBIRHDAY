a letter that was never really finished

Eashra

Happy Birthday — from someone who never really left.

scroll

Hi Eashra… it’s me, Farabi.
I don’t even know if you’ll read this, or if you’ll believe a single word I say anymore… but today is your birthday, and no matter how much time has passed, tomar kotha amar mone ekhono ager motoi ache.

Happy Birthday, jaan. ❤️

Aajke tomake wish korte giye bujhte parlam, kichu manush theke dure jawa jay… kintu mon theke dure jawa jay na.
Ami ekhono tomake miss kori. Khub beshi.
And yes… I still love you.

Hoyto tumi eta believe korbe na. Hoyto tomar kache amar bhalobasha ekhon shudhu kichu purono kotha. But amar kache tumi kokhono “past” hoye jao ni.

Ami tomake chere jai ni, Eashra.
Ami kokhono chere jete chai ni.
Shudhu circumstances amader moddhe emon ekta distance baniye dilo, je distance-ta ami kokhono bujhe uthte parini. And every day that passed without you somehow made that distance feel even bigger.

· · ·

Onek din hoye geche tomake dekhi na.
Onek din hoye geche tomar shathe kotha boli na.
But kichu kichu raat ekhono emon jay, jokhon tomake khub mone pore… and I quietly wonder, “Tumi ki amake ekbar-o mone koro?”

Ami jani ami perfect chilam na. Amar emotions ami shobshomoy thikmoto bujhte pari ni, express korte pari ni. Sometimes ami nijer moddhei hariye jai.
But amar ekta feeling kokhono change hoyni—
Ami tomake onek chai.
Tomar shathe kotha bolte chai.
Tomake ekbar dekhte chai.
Shudhu ekbar tomar shamne bose, kono explanation chara, shob kichu bhule kotha bolte chai.

I don’t want to force my way back into your life.
I don’t want to make you feel guilty.
I just want you to know that I never stopped caring.

· · ·

Maybe amar jayga tomar life-e ar nei…
Maybe tumi amake onek agei bhule gecho…
But amar mon ekhono shei jaygay dariye ache, jekhane amader kotha shesh hoyechilo.

And honestly, jaan…
ami shudhu chai tumi ekbar amar kotha bujho.

I’m sorry for everything.
I’m sorry for the things I couldn’t explain.
I’m sorry for the distance.
And I’m sorry that I couldn’t be there the way I wanted to be.

I still love you.
Maybe that’s the one thing I never learned how to stop doing.

Happy Birthday, my love. 🎂❤️
Tomar birthday-te amar ekta wish-i thakbe—
Jeno tumi shobshomoy happy thako.
Jeno tomar chokhe kokhono kono dukkho na thake.
And jodi konodin amar kotha tomar mone pore…
I hope you remember that somewhere, someone genuinely loved you, missed you, and never really stopped wishing that things could have been different.

Happy Birthday, Eashra.

I miss you. More than these words could ever explain.

— Farabi
